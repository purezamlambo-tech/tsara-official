About Me

I am Tsara, a South African music producer, DJ, sound designer, and audio engineer based in Dublin, Ireland. My musical identity is deeply rooted in Amapiano, Afro House, and African electronic music.

With formal training through a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Modern Music at BIMM Dublin, my work spans music production, composition, vocal processing, sound synthesis, audio editing, and live performance.

Through my weekly We Play Good MusiQ radio show on Dublin Digital Radio (90+ episodes), I promote musical discovery and cultural exchange. Having performed at prominent live venues and festivals across the circuit, I focus on delivering immersive sonic experiences for audiences, recording artists, and media.
