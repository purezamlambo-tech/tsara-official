About Me

I am Tsara, a South African music producer, DJ, and sound designer based in Dublin, Ireland. My musical identity is deeply rooted in Amapiano, Afro House, and modern African electronic music.

Currently studying Modern Music at BIMM Dublin, my work covers music production, composition, vocal tracking, sound design, audio editing, and live DJ sets. Through my weekly We Play Good MusiQ radio show on Dublin Digital Radio, I promote musical discovery, cultural exchange, and platform opportunities for emerging artists.
