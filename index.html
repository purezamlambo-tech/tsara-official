Biography

About Tsara

Peacemore Magonya, professionally known as Tsara, is a Dublin-based South African music producer, DJ, sound designer, and audio engineer. His musical identity is deeply rooted in Amapiano, Afro House, and contemporary African electronic music, combining traditional African musical influences with modern studio production, sound design, and live performance.

Alongside studying BA (Hons) in Modern Music at BIMM Dublin, Tsara produces original tracks, hosts the weekly We Play Good MusiQ show on Dublin Digital Radio, and performs across digital and live stages. His practice spans composition, arrangement, audio editing, vocal production, mixing, sound synthesis, and immersive soundscapes.

Driven by a vision to build a sustainable international career, Tsara focuses on releasing high-impact original music, collaborating with recording artists, and engineering audio for film, video games, interactive media, and digital platforms.
