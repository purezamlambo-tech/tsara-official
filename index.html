About Me

I am Tsara (Peacemore Magonya), a South African music producer, DJ, sound designer, and audio engineer based in Dublin, Ireland. My musical identity is deeply rooted in Amapiano, Afro House, and other forms of African electronic music.

Currently pursuing my Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Modern Music at BIMM Dublin, my work spans music production, composition, arrangement, recording and processing vocals, sound synthesis, audio editing, and live DJ performance.

Through my weekly We Play Good MusiQ radio show on Dublin Digital Radio, I promote musical discovery, cultural exchange, and platform opportunities for established and emerging artists. My long-term goal is to build a sustainable international career, producing original African electronic music and creating audio for recording artists, film, games, and interactive media.
