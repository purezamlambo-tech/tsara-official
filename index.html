About Me

I am Tsara, a South African music producer, DJ, sound designer, and creative technology practitioner based in Dublin, Ireland. My musical identity is deeply rooted in Amapiano, Afro House, and modern electronic music culture.

Currently pursuing studies in Modern Music at BIMM Dublin alongside AI workflow integration, my work spans music production, arrangement, vocal tracking, sound design, audio editing, and live performance. Through my weekly We Play Good MusiQ radio show on Dublin Digital Radio, I promote musical discovery and cultural exchange.
